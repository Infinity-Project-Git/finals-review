Final Exam Study Guide

Pick a subject. Do one short round whenever you can. No pressure, every round counts.

Just start one quick round. Five questions, about two minutes. Do one and you are done, that counts. Want more? Keep going. Flashcards let you flip at your own pace, and Answers is the full key (good for printing, or for someone to quiz you).

A few questions are about your own work (your project drug, your own coping ideas). Those are tagged YOUR ANSWER, only you can fill those in.
